FAQ

Questions, answered.

How much does a landing page cost?

A one-page launch site starts at $50 (or ₨5,000 for local clients). Personal portfolios start at $75, and multi-page business sites at $120. Use the estimator above for an instant quote — no call required.

How long does it take to build a website?

Most sites are delivered in about 5 days from the day we agree on the design direction. Need it faster? Priority delivery is available for a small rush fee.

Why a static website instead of WordPress?

Static sites load faster, can't be hacked through plugins, and need zero maintenance — no updates, no hosting headaches. If your site doesn't need logins or a database, static is simply the better engineering choice.

Do you work with international clients?

Yes. International projects are priced in USD starting at $50, and everything runs async over email — send a brief, get a free homepage preview, approve, and launch.

What is included in the price?

Every project includes strategy and page structure, a custom responsive design (no templates), the full front-end build, and basic search-engine setup. Two revision rounds are part of the process.
