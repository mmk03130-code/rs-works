Our story

One grill. One recipe book. One promise.

Zafrana is what happens when a family refuses to let its food be forgotten.

From Kabul to Lewisham

The food we grew up on, cooked the way we grew up with it.

Every family has a person whose cooking holds the family together. In ours it was a grandmother in Kabul whose mantu folds, saffron rice and green chutney set the standard everything else got measured against.

When the family settled in London, those recipes came with us — written in a school notebook, argued over at every family meal, and refined for a decade in home kitchens before Zafrana ever opened its doors on Lewisham High Street.

The rule from that notebook still runs the kitchen: charcoal, not gas, for the grill. Naan baked through service, never before it. Rice in small batches. If a dish can't be made properly, it doesn't go on the menu.

01

Charcoal only

Small batches

Guests, not orders

Taste it, don't take our word for it

The proof is on the plate.

Start with the kabuli pilaw and a skewer of seekh kebab. If those two plates don't bring you back, the next chapter of this story isn't ours to write.
