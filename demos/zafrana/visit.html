Good to know

Questions, answered

Is everything halal?

Yes — 100% of what we serve is fully halal, with no exceptions. It's the first promise we made and the one we keep closest.

Do you do takeaway and delivery?

Yes. Message us on WhatsApp to order takeaway, ready in about 20 minutes. Delivery runs within 3 miles for £2.50.

Can you cater my event?

Yes — weddings, office lunches and family gatherings are a regular part of our week. Send us the date, headcount and budget on WhatsApp and we'll build a spread around it.

Do you have vegetarian options?

Several: ashak, borani badenjan, lubia, daal, bolani and all our breads and salads are vegetarian.

Is there parking nearby?

On-street parking runs along the High Street and the surrounding roads; Lewisham station (DLR, Overground, buses) is a short walk away.
