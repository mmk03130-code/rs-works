Our Story

A six-table grill that grew up.

Saffron House started in 2019 as a six-table grill corner in Gulberg, with one wok, one tandoor, and a family recipe for karahi that regulars still ask about by name. Seven years later the room is bigger and the menu runs from smoky desi BBQ to continental grills — but every plate still leaves the kitchen the way it did on day one: sizzling, generous, and made to be shared.
